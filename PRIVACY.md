# CrawlVault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

CrawlVault is a browser extension that backs up Google Search Console crawl stats. It is built so that your data never leaves your browser.

## What CrawlVault reads

- Your list of Search Console properties and the Crawl stats report for the properties you back up. These are read from `search.google.com` using the Google session you are already signed in with in Chrome.
- Files you choose to import (Search Console CSV/zip exports or CrawlVault JSON backups).

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and `chrome.storage.local`). You can delete a property's data from the dashboard at any time, and uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

## What CrawlVault does not do

- It has no server. It does not send your data to The SEO Central or any third party.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.
- It does not read your Google password or any page other than Search Console.

The extension pages load the Inter and Poppins fonts from Google Fonts. Google may see that request as with any website font.
